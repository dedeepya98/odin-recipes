Description

This is a classic Italian dish that is perfect for any occasion. With layers of pasta, cheese, and rich meat sauce, it's sure to please everyone at the table.

Lets get started with a couple of key ingredients

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 (24-ounce) jar marinara sauce
  • 1 (15-ounce) container ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 12 lasagna noodles, cooked and drained
  • Salt and pepper to taste
